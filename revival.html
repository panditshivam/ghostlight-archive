    Behind the haunting · what actually runs

    The clues are an authored fictional puzzle. Solving them collects source material; it does not perform a server repair. The restore button asks permission to repair the supplied exhibit. Mastra tools run inside a real Temporal workflow on Render. Blowing out the candle terminates that isolated worker, not anything on your device. The replacement verifies existing output before adopting it.

    Room restoration and the ending wait for verified server outputs. Wisp’s ordinary dialogue is scripted fiction. The optional “Ask Wisp” route performs actual Gemma semantic matching, separately from those scripted lines. Sound is synthesized locally after your choice.

    One active restoration at a time. Free hosting can sleep. Runs and storage are temporary; keep a receipt if you want a lasting record. Recovery from worker death does not establish recovery after the whole hosting instance is lost.
